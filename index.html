About CryptoPulse AI

CryptoPulse AI is a browser-based cryptocurrency market information and learning platform created by Cornelius Aurelius. It combines third-party market quotes with transparent risk heuristics, a watchlist, price alerts that run while the website is open and a simulated portfolio using fictional funds. It does not place trades, hold real assets or provide financial advice. Generative AI research requires a separately configured secure backend and is not falsely represented as active on this static website.

How to use: open Market explorer to compare coins; select a market to read its data and source; save a watchlist; create in-tab price alerts; or use the Practice portfolio to simulate trades with an initial $10,000 fictional balance. If data cannot be verified, no substitute or made-up prices are shown.
